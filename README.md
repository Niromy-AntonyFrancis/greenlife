# 🌱 Green Life – Smart Sustainability Mobile App

**Green Life** is a smart sustainability mobile app designed to encourage individuals and communities to adopt eco-friendly habits and take meaningful action to protect the environment.

## 🌍 Problem

People are aware of climate change, pollution, and waste management issues, but they often lack a simple and engaging way to practice sustainable habits in their daily lives.

## 💡 Solution

Green Life helps users:

* 🌱 Track eco-friendly habits
* ♻️ Sort and dispose of waste correctly
* 🌍 Learn about environmental issues
* 🤝 Join community environmental events
* 📊 Track their carbon footprint
* 🏆 Earn points and rewards for sustainable actions

## ✨ Key Features

* 🌱 **Eco Habit Tracker** – Track sustainable daily activities and earn Eco Points.
* ♻️ **Smart Waste Sorting Guide** – Learn how to identify and properly dispose of different types of waste.
* 🌎 **Climate Awareness Hub** – Learn about climate change, pollution, biodiversity, floods, and droughts.
* 🤝 **Community Events** – Join cleanups, tree planting programs, school campaigns, and environmental activities.
* 📊 **Carbon Footprint Calculator** – Monitor environmental impact from electricity, fuel, and plastic usage.
* 🏆 **Reward System** – Earn Eco Points, badges, and sustainability achievements.

## 👥 Target Users

* Students and schools
* Families
* Local communities
* Environmental organizations and NGOs
